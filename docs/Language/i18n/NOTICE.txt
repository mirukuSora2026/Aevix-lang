Aevix Korean documentation translation credits

The initial Korean draft was generated locally with the Helsinki-NLP
OPUS-MT English-to-Korean model, then combined with reviewed terminology
and manual translations. No third-party translation service is used by
the documentation website. Model weights are not included in Aevix.

Model: Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-tc-big-en-ko
Developer: Language Technology Research Group, University of Helsinki
Revision: ae8606b7b29a495f31ce679cee2007f536a3a5ce
Model card:
https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-tc-big-en-ko/blob/ae8606b7b29a495f31ce679cee2007f536a3a5ce/README.md
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: Local int8 conversion; separate source and target tokenizer
vocabularies reconstructed from the pinned original SentencePiece IDs;
sentence splitting; identifier protection; terminology corrections;
manual translations and subsequent edits to the generated draft.
Original model weights were not changed.

The tokenizer correction addresses the upstream source/target vocabulary
mismatch described at:
https://github.com/Helsinki-NLP/OPUS-MT-train/issues/81
https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-tc-big-en-ko/discussions/7
